# Provider sign-in artwork

All assets are bundled locally; no third-party asset request or provider SDK is needed on the document-processing page. Provider names and artwork remain their owners’ trademarks and must be used according to their guidelines.

## Google

`google-sign-in-light.png` is Google's unmodified, pre-approved light rectangular
**Sign in with Google** button for Android and Web. It is stored at 4× resolution
(720 × 160) and displayed at 180 × 40 CSS pixels, preserving its aspect ratio.
Its text and logo are already rendered, so no external font is needed.

Source: [Google's Sign in with Google branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines),
downloaded 2026-10-25 from the linked
[asset archive](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip).
Archive entry:
`Android + Web/PNG @4x/Light/Theme=Light, Show text=Yes, Shape=Square, Platform=Android+Web@4x.png`.

Google owns this branding artwork; use it according to those guidelines.
The surrounding native button supplies its accessible name through the image's
alt text, keyboard focus, disabled state, and the existing OAuth action.


## Microsoft

`microsoft-sign-in-light.svg` is Microsoft's unmodified 215 × 41 light **Sign in with Microsoft** button.

Source: [Microsoft sign-in branding](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/howto-add-branding-in-apps),
[official SVG](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/media/howto-add-branding-in-apps/ms-symbollockup_signin_light.svg).

## Facebook (Meta)

`facebook-icon.png` is Facebook's unmodified 325 × 325 “f” logo, displayed at 24 × 24 with **Continue with Facebook**. It is not redrawn or recolored.

Source: [Facebook Login user experience](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/facebook-login/userexperience),
[official logo](https://www.facebook.com/images/fb_icon_325x325.png).

## Amazon

`amazon-login.png` is Amazon's unmodified 312 × 64 dark gray **Login with Amazon** button, displayed at 195 × 40 to preserve its proportions.

Source: [Login with Amazon buttons](https://developer.amazon.com/docs/login-with-amazon/button.html),
[official PNG](https://images-na.ssl-images-amazon.com/images/G/01/lwa/btnLWA_drkgry_312x64.png).
